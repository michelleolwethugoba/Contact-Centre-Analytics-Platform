<h5>User Story 1</h5>
<p>As an Operations Manager,</p>
<p>I want to view daily call volumes,</p>
<p>so that I can understand call demand.</p>

<h5>User Story 2</h5>
<p>As a Team Leader,</p>
<p>I want to identify agents with high missed call rates,</p>
<p>so that I can provide coaching.</p>

<h5>User Story 3</h5>
<p>As a Business Analyst,</p>
<p>I want to analyse trends in call volumes,</p>
<p>so that I can recommend process improvements.</p>

<h5>User Story 4</h5>
<p>As a Contact Centre Manager,</p>
<p>I want to monitor KPIs through dashboards,</p>
<p>so that I can make data-driven decisions.</p>
